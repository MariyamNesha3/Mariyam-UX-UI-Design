The Challenges

Identifying key workflow and usability challenges to create a centralized, efficient, and easy-to-manage financial dashboard for teams handling clients, banking information, tasks, documents, and communication.

Keeping Financial
Tasks Organized

Managing daily tasks, follow-ups, and deadlines can become difficult when information is spread across multiple tools. The dashboard needed to make priorities and pending work easy to track.

Managing Multiple
Client Details

Financial teams often handle client information across different records and activities. The challenge was bringing client details, banking information, and important updates into one organized workspace.

Handling Documents
Efficiently

Financial operations involve multiple documents and records that need to be accessed and managed regularly. The challenge was creating a clear structure for finding, reviewing, and organizing important files.

Clear Communication
& Team Coordination

Teams need to stay aligned on client updates, tasks, and financial activities. The challenge was supporting smooth communication and coordination without making the dashboard feel overwhelming.

Mapping the Experience

The experience map connects client management, banking operations, task tracking, document handling, and team communication to create a seamless and efficient financial workflow.

Building the Digital Experience

A centralized dashboard that gives users a quick overview of tasks, activity, upcoming work, team members, and important updates in one place.
