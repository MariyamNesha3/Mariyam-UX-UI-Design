Bio

Sneha Verma is a young working professional who uses astrology apps for daily guidance, relationship insights, and important life decisions. She prefers quick access to trustworthy astrologers and simple, easy-to-understand astrology services.

Core Needs
  • Find trusted astrologers based on expertise, ratings, and pricing.
  • Get quick access to daily horoscope and personalized predictions.
  • View Kundli and matchmaking information easily.
  • Compare astrologers before starting a consultation.
  • Book or start a chat/call without a complicated process.
Frustrations
  • Too many services and options can make navigation confusing.
  • Difficulty identifying reliable astrologers.
  • Complex Kundli information is hard to understand.
  • Unclear consultation pricing or charges.
  • Too many promotional elements competing for attention.
