UI Design

A collection of the main website interfaces, showcasing the homepage, product and solution pages, packages, about us, and enquiry experience.

The aerial hero imagery immediately communicates TASK’s industry and establishes a strong visual introduction to the platform.
Products and solutions are presented with clear categories and supporting visuals, making complex technical services easier to understand.
The footer brings together important navigation links, product and solution categories, company information, and contact details for easy access.
The navigation keeps Products, Solutions, Packages, and About Us easily accessible, helping users explore the website without confusion.
The “Why TASK?” section highlights the platform’s key capabilities and builds trust by clearly communicating its value.
Prominent “Make an Enquiry” and “Try Now” CTAs guide users toward taking the next step after exploring TASK’s offerings.

Products

The product pages present TASK’s three core capabilities—Acquisition, Processing, and Analytics—with dedicated layouts that explain each offering, highlight key features, showcase supporting visuals, and guide users toward taking the next step.

Solutions

The solution pages showcase TASK’s industry-focused offerings across Power Distribution, Solar Asset Management, and Highway Management, explaining the challenges, proposed solutions, key benefits, and results through structured content and visuals.

The About Us page introduces Trentar, its group companies, services, expertise, and key strengths to build trust and credibility.
The Packages page presents different plans with feature comparisons, helping users understand the available options and choose the right package.
The Enquiry page provides a simple contact form where users can share their requirements and connect with the TASK team.
