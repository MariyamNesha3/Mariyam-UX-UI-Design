Empathy Map

Understanding users’ needs, motivations, frustrations, and behaviors to create a more simple, engaging, and user-friendly anonymous messaging experience.

👣DOES

  • Creates a personal profile and anonymous message link.
  • Shares the link with friends through social media.
  • Checks the message box for new anonymous messages.
  • Reads, replies to, and manages received messages.
  • Shares their profile or message link on Instagram and WhatsApp.

😣PAINS

  • Difficulty getting friends to respond.
  • Unclear instructions for creating and sharing a profile.
  • Confusing or lengthy sharing process.
  • Concerns about privacy and anonymous messages.
  • Too many steps to access or manage messages.

🧠THINKS

  • “Who could have sent me this message?”
  • “Will my friends actually send me messages?”
  • “Is my identity safe?”
  • “How can I get more responses?”
  • “I want to easily share my profile with friends.”

❤️FEELS

  • Curious about what friends think of them.
  • Excited when they receive a new message.
  • Anxious about potentially negative messages.
  • Comfortable when privacy is clearly protected.
  • Engaged when the experience feels social and fun.

💬SAYS

  • “I want to know what my friends really think.”
  • “I hope I get some interesting messages.”
  • “I want the sharing process to be quick.”
  • “I don’t want others to know who sent the message.”

✨GAINS

  • Easy way to receive honest anonymous feedback.
  • Fun way to interact with friends.
  • Simple profile creation and link sharing.
  • Quick access to received messages.
  • Easy sharing across social platforms.
  • Greater confidence through friends’ opinions.

UI Design

A collection of key interfaces designed for SecretNote, showcasing message creation, sharing, anonymous interactions, friend messages, and the overall user experience.

The experience focuses on making anonymous messaging easy, social, and engaging. Users can create their personal message link, share it with friends, receive anonymous messages, and manage replies from one simple interface. Supporting sections such as blogs, trending content, and social sharing encourage continued engagement while keeping the primary messaging flow clear.
