BFF Test

Product Overview

BFF Test is a fun friendship quiz app designed to help friends discover how well they know each other.

Target Player

BFF quiz apps primarily attract socially active users who enjoy testing friendships, discovering how well their friends know them, and sharing fun results with their friend groups.

55%

Young Adults
(13–24 Years)

Enjoy quick friendship quizzes, fun challenges, and shareable results. They are highly likely to use quizzes as a social activity with friends and in group chats.

30%

15%

The Challenges

The main challenge was creating a quiz experience that felt entertaining while remaining simple enough for users to understand immediately.

Making the Quiz
Feel Fun

Create a playful experience that makes users excited to take a friendship test without making the interface feel overwhelming.

Keeping the Quiz
Simple

Users should be able to understand the question, select an answer, and move to the next step without confusion or unnecessary interactions.

Balancing Content
& Visuals

Use illustrations, colors, cards, and playful elements to give the app personality while keeping the questions and actions easy to focus on.

Making Results
Shareable

The result should feel personal and exciting enough that users want to share their friendship score with their friends.
