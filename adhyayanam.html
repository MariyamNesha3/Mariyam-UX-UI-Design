Bio

Rohan is a final-year BCA student preparing for NIMCET alongside his college studies. He uses mobile apps to access study materials, practice mock tests, and track his preparation. He prefers a simple and organized platform that helps him identify weak areas and improve his performance.

Core Needs
  • Access NIMCET study materials in one place.
  • Practice with mock tests and previous-year questions.
  • Track scores, accuracy, rank, and progress.
  • Understand mistakes through detailed solutions.
  • Identify weak subjects and topics.
  • Manage preparation efficiently alongside college.
Frustrations
  • Study materials are scattered across different platforms.
  • Difficult to track overall preparation progress.
  • Limited performance insights after mock tests.
  • Repeatedly making mistakes without understanding them.
  • Difficulty finding reliable and relevant NIMCET resources.

Empathy Map

Understanding students’ thoughts, emotions, behaviors, and challenges to create a more focused and effective NIMCET preparation experience.

👣DOES

  • Studies NIMCET-related subjects through mobile resources.
  • Practices mock tests and previous-year questions.
  • Reviews incorrect answers and solutions.
  • Checks scores, rank and percentile after tests.
  • Revisits difficult topics and study materials.

😣PAINS

  • Difficulty finding reliable study material.
  • Limited understanding of weak subjects and topics.
  • Lack of detailed performance insights.
  • Difficulty maintaining consistent preparation.
  • Repeated mistakes without clear explanations.
  • Uncertainty about exam readiness.

🧠THINKS

  • “Am I preparing enough for NIMCET?”
  • “Which subjects should I focus on more?”
  • “How am I performing compared to other students?”
  • “Can I improve my score before the exam?”
  • “Which topics do I need to practice again?”

❤️FEELS

  • Motivated when seeing improvement in scores.
  • Anxious about exam preparation and competition.
  • Frustrated when mistakes are repeated.
  • Confident when performance is clearly tracked.
  • Overwhelmed when study resources are scattered.

💬SAYS

  • “I want all my study material in one place.”
  • “I need more NIMCET mock tests.”
  • “I want to understand where I’m making mistakes.”
  • “The test results should show my actual performance.”
  • “I need a simple way to track my preparation.”

✨GAINS

  • Centralized study materials and resources.
  • Regular mock-test practice.
  • Clear score, rank and percentile tracking.
  • Detailed solutions to understand mistakes.
  • Better understanding of strengths and weaknesses.
  • More confidence and structured NIMCET preparation.
