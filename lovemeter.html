Lovemeter

Product Overview

Lovemeter is a fun, interactive friendship and prank quiz experience designed to let users create personalized love-meter links, share them with friends, and reveal playful results through engaging quizzes.

UI Design

A collection of the key Lovemeter screens, showcasing the prank creation flow, LoveMeter experience, sharing options, Prankboard, and interactive results.

The Lovemeter Prank is the main highlight, placed prominently at the top to immediately guide users toward creating a fun prank.
The footer provides quick access to social sharing, essential links, and advertisement space without interrupting the main experience.
The clear Start button provides an easy entry point, allowing users to begin the Lovemeter experience with minimal effort.
The Blogs section adds supporting content while keeping users engaged beyond the main prank experience.
The experience smoothly guides users from creating a personalized LoveMeter prank to sharing it with friends. The opening screen clearly introduces the prank and provides a simple name field and Create Link CTA to get started. The How to Play section explains the prank flow, while the Blogs section provides additional content. Once the link is created, users can easily copy it or share it across multiple social platforms. The Prankboard lets users view submitted results, while the Genuine Love Meter CTA provides an additional path to discover their own love percentage.
The prominent sharing options make it easy to copy and share the prank link across multiple social platforms.
The Prankboard gives users a quick view of friends’ responses and love-meter results in one place.
The Genuine Love Meter CTA provides a clear next step for users who want to discover their own love percentage.
The simple input screen lets users enter their name and crush’s name to start the LoveMeter experience. The clear CTA keeps the process quick and easy.
The result screen presents the love percentage clearly, with options to replay or create a new prank link and share the experience with friends.
