The Challenges

Identifying key usability and workflow challenges to create a clear, efficient, and reliable financial management experience for teams handling clients, banking data, tasks, documents, and communication.

Keeping Everything
in One Place

Teams often work across client records, bank details, tasks, files, and messages. The challenge was creating a centralized dashboard that reduces unnecessary switching between different workflows.

Managing Complex
Information

Users need to manage large amounts of client, banking, and document data. The challenge was presenting detailed information in a structured way without overwhelming the user.

Clear Status &
Progress Visibility

Users need to understand what has been completed, what requires attention, and which tasks are still pending. The challenge was creating clear status indicators and progress feedback.

Simplifying Daily
Operations

Users need to quickly add clients, upload documents, manage bank information, and track assigned tasks. The challenge was making these frequent actions simple and efficient.
