Bio

Rahul is a working professional who uses his car regularly for commuting and personal travel. He prefers convenient car servicing that saves time and allows him to manage the service without visiting a workshop multiple times.

Core Needs
  • Book reliable car services easily.
  • Compare services and pricing before booking.
  • Schedule convenient pickup and drop-off.
  • Track the vehicle service progress.
  • Receive clear updates throughout the service.
Frustrations
  • Spending time visiting workshops for service.
  • Unclear service pricing or additional charges.
  • Difficulty knowing the vehicle’s service status.
  • Delays in pickup or vehicle delivery.
  • Lack of clear communication from the workshop.

Empathy Map

Understanding customers’ behaviors, thoughts, and challenges to create a convenient and reliable car-service experience.

👣DOES

  • Uses the app to find and book car services.
  • Checks service packages and pricing.
  • Schedules pickup and drop-off.
  • Tracks the vehicle during servicing.
  • Reviews service details and updates.

😣PAINS

  • Time-consuming workshop visits.
  • Unclear pricing or additional charges.
  • Delays in pickup or delivery.
  • Limited visibility into service progress.
  • Difficulty communicating with the service center.

🧠THINKS

  • “Is this service reliable?”
  • “How much will the service cost?”
  • “When will my car be picked up?”
  • “What is happening with my car?”
  • “When will I get my car back?”

❤️FEELS

  • Confident when the service process is clear.
  • Relieved when pickup and drop are handled.
  • Anxious when there are no service updates.
  • Frustrated by unexpected charges or delays.
  • Satisfied when the car is returned on time.

💬SAYS

  • “I want the pricing to be clear.”
  • “I don’t have time to visit a workshop.”
  • “I want to know my car’s service status.”
  • “Pickup and drop should be convenient.”
  • “I want a reliable service experience.”

✨GAINS

  • Convenient doorstep pickup and drop.
  • Transparent service pricing.
  • Real-time service updates.
  • Saves time and reduces workshop visits.
  • Reliable and hassle-free car servicing.
