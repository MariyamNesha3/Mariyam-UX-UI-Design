AMS Hospital

Product Overview

AMS Hospitals is a healthcare platform with a patient-facing website and employee dashboard, designed to simplify hospital services and daily internal workflows.

Role

The Challenges

Creating a clear healthcare experience that helps patients quickly find relevant information, understand available services, and complete important actions with ease.

Finding Information
Quickly

Patients need to easily discover doctors, medical services, facilities, and health checkup options. The challenge was to create a clear structure that makes important information easy to find.

Simplifying a Complex
Healthcare Website

AMS offers multiple specialties, treatments, services, and facilities. The challenge was to organize this information into a simple and intuitive experience without overwhelming users.

Designing for Patients &
Employees

The platform serves both patients through the hospital website and employees through an internal dashboard. The challenge was to create focused experiences that support different user needs and workflows.

UI Design

A collection of key hospital interfaces, showcasing the website, service pages, doctor information, appointment flow, and employee dashboard for a seamless healthcare experience.

Key actions like Book an Appointment, Our Brochure, and Health Checkup Plan are placed prominently for quick access.
Medical, maternity, surgical, and health checkup services are presented as clear sections to help users quickly find the care they need.
Hospital information, testimonials, events, and insurance partners are structured throughout the page to build credibility and support informed decisions.
The Events section highlights hospital activities and updates, helping visitors stay informed about ongoing events and announcements.
