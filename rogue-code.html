A collection of the key interfaces across the Rogue Code platform, showcasing course discovery, learning, coding practice, progress tracking, interview preparation, and student interactions.

Usability Testing

Evaluated key student interactions, including course discovery, learning, coding practice, progress tracking, and interview preparation, to identify usability issues and improve the overall learning experience.

Clear Course Discovery

Students need a simple course structure and clear information to quickly understand where to start and what to learn next.

Easy Coding Practice

The online compiler should be easy to access and use, allowing students to practice coding without switching between different platforms.

Smooth Learning Progress

Clear progress tracking and organized interview preparation help students understand their improvement and stay motivated throughout their learning journey.
