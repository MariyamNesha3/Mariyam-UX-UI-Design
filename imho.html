Bio

Riya is a college student who enjoys connecting with friends, sharing opinions, and trying fun social apps. She likes anonymous Q&A because it lets her express herself and discover what her friends really think.

Core Needs
  • Ask and receive questions anonymously.
  • Share her IMHO profile easily with friends.
  • Discover honest opinions and fun responses.
  • Reply to questions without a complicated flow.
  • Feel safe while interacting anonymously.
Frustrations
  • Repetitive or boring questions.
  • Confusing navigation between questions and responses.
  • Unclear anonymity and privacy controls.
  • Spam or unwanted messages.
  • Too many steps to share or respond.
Preferred Brands

Instagram | Snapchat | WhatsApp | Spotify

Empathy Map

👣DOES

😣PAINS

  • Repetitive or boring questions.
  • Unwanted or inappropriate messages.
  • Uncertainty about who sent a message.

  • Concerns about privacy and anonymous interactions.

  • “What do my friends really think about me?”

  • “Is my identity protected?”

❤️FEELS

  • Curious when receiving anonymous questions.
  • Excited when friends respond.
  • Comfortable expressing thoughts anonymously.
  • Frustrated by spam or unwanted messages.
  • Concerned about privacy and safety.

💬SAYS

  • “I want honest answers from my friends.”
  • “Anonymous questions are more fun.”
  • “I wish I knew who sent this.”
  • “Sharing my profile should be easy.”
  • “I want to control who can interact with me.”

✨GAINS

  • Honest and fun conversations with friends.
  • Easy way to ask and answer anonymously.
  • More engaging social interactions.
  • Quick sharing across social platforms.
