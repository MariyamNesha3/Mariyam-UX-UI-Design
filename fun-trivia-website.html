Fun Trivia Website

Product Overview

A fun friendship trivia website designed to help friends test how well they know each other through playful questions, challenges, and interactive quizzes.

Tools
Role
UX/UI DESIGNER | GRAPHIC DESIGNER
Focus
UX Architecture | Quiz Experience | User Flow | Visual Design | Interactive Design

Target Player

BFF trivia websites mainly attract socially active users who enjoy testing their friendships, discovering how well their friends know them, and sharing funny quiz results with their friend groups.

55%

Young Adults
(13–24 Years)

Enjoy quick friendship quizzes, fun challenges, and shareable results. They are highly likely to use quizzes as a social activity with friends and in group chats.

30%

Adults
(25–34 Years)

Prefer entertaining quizzes that help them compare friendships, revisit shared memories, and create fun moments with close friends.

15%

Mature Adults
(35+ Years)

Use friendship quizzes mainly for casual entertainment and social interaction, especially when playing with close friends or family.

Mapping the Experience

Mapping the player journey from creating or joining a BFF quiz to answering questions, completing the challenge, and discovering friendship results.

Building the Website

Create a fun and engaging friendship-trivia experience that balances simple interactions, playful visuals, and an easy quiz flow to keep users engaged from start to finish.

UI Design

A collection of the main website screens, showcasing the quiz experience, homepage, quiz selection, question screens, answer interactions, friendship results, score comparison, and sharing features.

Quiz Cards

The featured BFF quiz cards are placed prominently at the top, making it easy for users to discover and start a friendship quiz.

Ads

Ads placements are integrated into the layout to support website monetization while keeping advertisements separate from the main quiz experience.

Footer

The footer provides quick access to social media, privacy policy, terms of use, FAQs, and other important website information.

Clear Navigation

The simple navigation and language options help users move through the website quickly and access important sections without confusion.

Blog

The blog section provides friendship-related content and keeps users engaged beyond the quiz experience, encouraging them to explore more of the website.
