Bio

Aarav is a young professional who commutes to work and visits nearby places in his free time. He prefers affordable, eco-friendly transportation and uses mobile apps to find convenient e-bicycles for everyday travel.

Core Needs
  • Find nearby available e-bicycles quickly.
  • Check battery level and estimated riding range.
  • View rental charges before starting a ride.
  • Locate bicycle stations and designated parking areas.
  • Unlock and return e-bicycles easily.
Frustrations
  • Nearby e-bicycles may be unavailable or have low battery.
  • Unclear rental pricing and unexpected charges.
  • Difficulty finding designated return and parking locations.
  • Uncertainty about battery range and ride completion.
Preferred Brands

Google Maps | Rapido | PhonePe | Uber

Empathy Map

Understanding users’ needs, motivations, frustrations, and behaviors to create a more convenient and user-friendly e-bike rental experience.

👣DOES

  • Uses the app to find nearby e-bicycles.
  • Checks availability, battery level and riding range.
  • Rents e-bicycles for commuting and short trips.
  • Follows parking rules and returns bicycles to designated zones.
  • Uses the app to make payments and view ride history.

😣PAINS

  • No available e-bicycles nearby.
  • Low battery or unclear riding range.
  • Hidden charges and confusing pricing.
  • Difficulty finding designated parking areas.
  • App errors or slow unlocking.
  • Poor bicycle maintenance or safety concerns.

🧠THINKS

  • “Is there an available e-bicycle near me?”
  • “How much will this ride cost?”
  • “Will the battery last for my trip?”
  • “Where can I safely return the bicycle?”
  • “Can I reach my destination on time?”

❤️FEELS

  • Happy and refreshed after a ride.
  • Confident when the app is easy to use.
  • Frustrated when bicycles are unavailable.
  • Worried about battery life and reaching the destination on time.
  • Motivated to choose sustainable transportation.

💬SAYS

  • “I hope there is a bicycle nearby.”
  • “The pricing should be clear.”
  • “I want to see the battery level before starting.”
  • “Unlocking and returning should be easy.”
  • “I want a smooth and safe ride.”

✨GAINS

  • Convenient and affordable last-mile transport.
  • Saves time and reduces travel stress.
  • Supports an eco-friendly lifestyle.
  • Incorporates light physical activity into daily travel.
  • Transparent pricing and reliable battery information.
  • Easy access to nearby e-bicycles.
  • Improved urban mobility and connectivity.

UI Design

A clear and intuitive interface designed to make bike discovery, booking, journey tracking, and payment simple and seamless.

Onboarding screens

Login/sign up

A simple and secure authentication flow designed to help users quickly access Rydio and start their e-bike journey.

Home

The home screen gives users a quick overview of nearby e-bikes and makes it easy to plan their next journey. The map-based interface helps users locate available rides, while destination search, ride details, and ride history make the rental experience simple and convenient.

About bike

Users get essential information about a selected e-bike, from pricing and battery availability to its recent journey history. Showing the bike’s previous trips gives users greater visibility into its usage while keeping booking information easily accessible.
Pick your bike

Journey

The journey flow makes starting and managing an e-bike ride simple. Users can scan the bike’s QR code, review the fare and battery status, confirm their booking, and monitor their active ride with real-time journey and package information.
Recharge

Payment

The payment interface keeps pricing and payment details clear, with a strong focus on the total amount and Pay CTA for a simple experience.
