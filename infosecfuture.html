InfoSec Future

Product Overview

InfoSec Future is a recruitment and talent management platform that connects employers, recruiters, and jobseekers through streamlined hiring, candidate discovery, job applications, and recruitment management.

The Challenges

Creating a seamless recruitment experience that helps employers, recruiters, and jobseekers easily find the right opportunities, manage applications, and connect with the right talent.

Finding the Right
Talent Quickly

Employers and recruiters need to discover suitable candidates without spending excessive time searching through profiles. The challenge was to create a clear experience that makes candidate discovery and hiring decisions faster.

Complex Recruitment
Platform

The platform serves employers, recruiters, and jobseekers with different goals and workflows. The challenge was to organize jobs, applications, candidates, referrals, and hiring activities into simple and intuitive experiences.

Multiple Users &
Workflows

Each user has different needs—from jobseekers applying for jobs to recruiters managing referrals and employers hiring candidates. The challenge was to create focused experiences while keeping the overall platform consistent and easy to navigate.

Mapping the Experience

Mapping the user’s journey across the platform to create a clear and intuitive recruitment experience for jobseekers, recruiters, and employers.

UI Design

A collection of key recruitment interfaces, showcasing the website, job search, candidate management, dashboards, and hiring workflows for a seamless talent experience.

The hero section immediately communicates the platform’s AI-powered talent solution and guides users toward hiring.
The clear navigation helps users quickly choose their path as an Employer, Recruiter, or Jobseeker.
The dedicated sections highlight key benefits for employers, recruiters, and jobseekers, making each user journey easy to understand.
The strong CTA and trust section encourage users to take the next step and connect with the right talent or opportunity.
